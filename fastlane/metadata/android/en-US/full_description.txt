Axis is a voxel game engine with multiplayer support, forked from Luanti.

It renders and simulates a near-infinite voxel world, runs dedicated servers, and exposes a Lua modding API for building games on top of it.

There is a wide range of built-in map generators for procedurally generated terrain, and voxel-based lighting that adapts to the environment and light sources.

Games and mods are written in Lua. Axis is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Luanti project.
